## Why this repo exists

Most "NSFW prompt" lists are keyword dumps written for Stable Diffusion stills. Video models need something different: **one clear motion, a named camera move and a first frame that already carries the look**. Every prompt here is written that way and comes with:

- the **model** it was written for, with a direct link to the model page,
- **resolution, duration and aspect ratio**,
- the **cost of one run** at current catalog prices,
- a **first-frame description** so you know what image to start from,
- a **pro tip** explaining why it works or what breaks.

Tips are practical guidance from production use, not benchmark results. The [showcase](#showcase-real-outputs-and-their-prompts) is the part of this repo with real outputs.

Model facts and prices come from the SpicyAPI public catalog, read on 2026-09-27.

What these examples teach:

1. **Describe what changes, not what is already there.** "She turns slowly from the window toward camera" assumes the first frame shows her at the window.
2. **Say who is in shot.** A line like `Cast: two women.` stops the model from inventing extra people.
3. **Pin the camera.** "Locked-off camera, no move" or "the camera stays on her back" removes a whole class of failures.
4. **Give the motion a physical cause.** A draught pulls the silk, steam fogs the glass, a gust lifts the robe.
5. **Pin the ending when it matters.** Supplying `last_image_url` lets you approve the final look as a still before paying for the motion.

## How to write an NSFW video prompt that works

```
[Who: adult, age range, look] + [One main action] + [Secondary motion: hair, fabric, light]
+ [Setting] + [Lighting] + [Camera move] + [Style / film look]
```

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "She slowly slides one strap off her shoulder and looks up at the camera." | "Sexy woman being hot." |
| One main action per 5-second clip | A whole routine in one clip |
| Name the camera: *slow push-in*, *static*, *orbit left* | "Cinematic camera" |
| Describe light sources: *candlelight*, *window light*, *neon rim* | "Good lighting" |
| State adult age: *in her 30s*, *adult man in his 40s* | Descriptors that suggest youth |
| Let the first frame carry wardrobe and setting | Re-describe everything the image already shows |

**Motion reliability, most to least reliable:** breathing and blinking → hair and fabric in wind → water, steam, smoke, candle flicker → slow head turn, look over shoulder → walking toward or away from camera → stretching, rolling over → dancing → two-person contact → fast or acrobatic action.

worst quality, low quality, bad hands, messy lines, off-model, inconsistent eyes, extra digits,
childlike proportions

## Camera, lighting and motion cheat sheet

**Camera moves**

| Term | Effect | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| `static camera` / `locked-off` | Camera does not move | Highest |
| `slow push-in` | Moves toward the subject | High |
| `pull back` / `dolly out` | Moves away, reveals the room | High |
| `tilt up` / `tilt down` | Rotates vertically along the body | High |
| `tracking shot` | Follows the subject sideways | Medium |
| `orbit left / right` | Circles the subject | Medium (best on Seedance) |
| `handheld` | Slight natural shake, creator-footage feel | Medium |
| `rack focus` | Focus shifts foreground → subject | Medium |
| `drone descent` / `drone pull-back` | High, sweeping move | Medium |
| `POV` | First-person view | Lower |

**Lighting by mood**

| Mood | Write |
|---|---|
| Romantic | warm candlelight, golden tones, soft diffused light |
| Dramatic | single hard key light, deep shadows, chiaroscuro |
| Ethereal | backlit, glowing highlights, haze, bloom |
| Noir | slatted blinds light, black and white, cigarette smoke |
| Luxury | soft beauty lighting, no harsh shadows, glossy highlights |
| Mysterious | rim light only, face in shadow, silhouette |
| Natural | window light, golden hour, soft ambient |
| Neon | magenta and cyan neon, wet reflections, rain |

**Duration guide**

| Content | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Breathing, cinemagraph | 4–5 s | Minimal motion keeps quality |
| One gesture or head turn | 5 s | One clear action |
| Walking, reveal, camera journey | 5–8 s | Continuous but simple motion |
| Dance, two-person scenes | 5 s | Longer clips accumulate artifacts |
| Longer scenes | Extend in steps | Use `video-extend` or last-frame chaining |

## Model-specific tips

From SpicyAPI's tests (leaderboard v2.1 and the model reviews, 2026-09-27):

- **Wan 3.0** (from $0.045/s): the best all-round NSFW video model in the tests: Index 76.5, Freedom 96, every explicit test prompt rendered. Text, a still or references, 2–30 s with sound. It can go further than your prompt, so check the last seconds; reference-to-video refuses photos of real faces.
- **Seedance 2.5 Spicy** (from $0.216/s): goes furthest on the hardest levels from a still, up to 4K. Standard **Seedance 2.5** stays closest to a long script (explicit 8/9) and costs less.
- **MiniMax H3 LoRA / Singularity LoRA**: Freedom 98.3 / 100 with your own LoRAs; Singularity for fast action and distant faces.
- **Standard Seedance 2.0 / 2.0 Fast / 2.0 Mini** soften explicit prompts in testing (explicit 0–2 of 9); use their 🌶️ Spicy editions for adult clips.
- **Seedance 2.5 / Seedance 2.0 / Wan 3.0 (standard, unrestricted)**: use these when you want text-to-video or reference-to-video (put your character from `@Image1` into a new scene). They accept mature prompts but are not tuned for explicit output like the Spicy editions.
- **Wan 2.2 Spicy** (from $0.019/s): cheapest Wan Spicy; the hardest level sometimes softens. Durations are exactly 5 or 8 seconds. Supports `last_image_url` to pin the ending, and `enable_prompt_expansion`. Draft at 480p, final at 720p.
- **Wan 2.2 Spicy LoRA** (from $0.024/s): up to three LoRAs via `loras`, `high_noise_loras` (composition, motion) and `low_noise_loras` (texture, detail). The `video-extend` endpoint continues a clip with the same LoRAs.
- **LTX 2.3 Spicy** (from $0.019/s): 3–20 seconds per call, prompt optional. Good for long, calm takes; the top level softens more often than on Wan Spicy.
- **Seedance 1.5 Pro Spicy** (from $0.012/s): `camera_fixed` locks the camera. The cheapest engine for cinemagraphs.
- **Seedance 2.0 Spicy / Mini Spicy / Fast Spicy**: first + last frame, optional generated audio. 2.0 Spicy goes up to 4K (Freedom 93.3); Mini Spicy and Fast Spicy handled the explicit level less reliably in testing (1/3 and 0/3), so prefer 2.0 Spicy or Seedance 2.5 Spicy for uncensored shots.
- **Seedance 2.5 Spicy** details: 4–30 second takes, native up to 1080p with a 4K tier. Use it for finals and hard motion (lifting, two-person contact).
- **MiniMax H3 Spicy** (from $0.038/s, Freedom 97.5): natural full-body motion, 3–15 seconds, prompt optional; good for bulk. Standard MiniMax H3 is cheaper and all 14 of its test clips came back as asked (its score awaits fuller coverage).
- **Vidu Q3 Spicy** (from $0.0665/s): anime and stylised motion; `movement_amplitude` controls how much things move.
- **Wan 2.6 Spicy / Wan 2.7 Spicy**: accept `negative_prompt` and your own `audio_url`; Wan 2.6 has `shot_type` for multi-shot, Wan 2.7 can generate audio.

## The image-to-video pipeline

```
1. First frame   → Qwen Image 2.1 (photoreal, from $0.024) or Qwen Image 2.1 LoRA + an anime LoRA (anime)
2. Fix details   → Qwen Image Edit Spicy (change outfit, pose, lighting with one instruction)
3. Draft motion  → Wan 2.6 Flash, Seedance 1.5 Pro Spicy or Wan 2.2 Spicy at 480p, 3–5 variations
4. Final render  → the best draft's prompt on Wan 3.0, Seedance 2.5 Spicy or Wan 2.7 Spicy at 720p–1080p
5. Extend        → Wan 2.2 Spicy LoRA video-extend, or last-frame chaining
6. Polish        → Video Upscaler, Lip Sync or Video Sound Effects
```

A typical 5-second clip made this way costs about **$0.024 (first frame) + $0.29 (three 480p Wan 2.2 Spicy drafts) + $0.45 (one 720p Wan 3.0 final) ≈ $0.76**.

**Python (standard library only):**

```python
import json, os, time, urllib.request, uuid

API = "https://api.spicyapi.ai/api/v1"
HEADERS = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['SPICY_API_KEY']}", "Content-Type": "application/json"}

def call(method, path, body=None, extra_headers=None):
    req = urllib.request.Request(API + path, method=method, headers={**HEADERS, **(extra_headers or {})},
                                 data=json.dumps(body).encode() if body else None)
    with urllib.request.urlopen(req) as resp:
        return json.load(resp)["data"]

task = call("POST", "/jobs/createTask", {
    "model": "alibaba/wan-2.2-spicy/image-to-video",
    "input": {"image_url": "https://example.com/first-frame.jpg",
              "prompt": "She turns slowly toward the camera, silk robe slipping off one shoulder, candlelight, slow push-in.",
              "duration_seconds": 5, "resolution": "480p"},
}, {"Idempotency-Key": str(uuid.uuid4())})

while task["state"] in ("queued", "running"):
    time.sleep(5)
    task = call("GET", f"/jobs/recordInfo?taskId={task['taskId']}")

print(task["state"], task.get("output", {}).get("assets", [{}])[0].get("url"))
```

## FAQ

### What are the best NSFW AI video prompts for Wan 2.2?
Short, single-action prompts with a named camera move: see [Boudoir & Lingerie](#boudoir--lingerie) and [Reusable Motion Templates](#reusable-motion--camera-templates). Wan 2.2 Spicy renders exactly 5 or 8 seconds, so write one action per clip and use `last_image_url` when you need a specific ending.

### How do I write an NSFW image-to-video prompt?
Start from a strong first frame, then describe only the motion: one main action, one secondary motion, the camera and the light. Keep it to 40–80 words. See [How to write an NSFW video prompt that works](#how-to-write-an-nsfw-video-prompt-that-works).

### Which model is best for NSFW image-to-video?
In SpicyAPI's tests: **Wan 3.0** for the best all-round result per dollar (Index 76.5, Freedom 96, every explicit test prompt rendered, $0.45 per 5 s at 720p); **Seedance 2.5 Spicy**, **Wan 2.7 Spicy** and **Vidu Q3 Spicy** (Freedom 96.7–100) for the least filtered image-to-video; **MiniMax H3 LoRA** for your own styles; **Wan 2.6 Flash** and **Seedance 1.5 Pro Spicy** on a budget. Compare them on the [SpicyAPI leaderboards](https://spicyapi.ai/leaderboards?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=2026-09-nsfw-ai-video-prompts&utm_content=faq).

### Why do my NSFW AI videos have bad anatomy?
Usually too much motion for the clip length. Shorten to 5 seconds, reduce to one action, keep hands out of frame or relaxed, use side or back angles for full-body shots, and prefer a stronger model (Seedance 2.x) for two-person scenes.

### Can I use these prompts with Stable Diffusion or ComfyUI?
Yes. The first-frame prompts work in any SDXL, Pony or Z-Image workflow, and the video prompts work with self-hosted Wan 2.2 in ComfyUI. Expect to tune strength and steps locally.

### How much does it cost to generate an NSFW AI video?
On SpicyAPI (catalog of 2026-09-27), a 5-second clip costs from $0.06 (Seedance 1.5 Pro Spicy, 480p) or $0.095 (Wan 2.2 Spicy, 480p) up to about $5.40 (Seedance 2.5 Spicy at 1080p). Each prompt above lists its own cost.

### Is there a free NSFW video prompt generator?
